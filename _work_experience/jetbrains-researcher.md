I joined JetBrains in September 2020 as a software engineering researcher, working on machine learning and empirical studies.
I initiated a research project on Jupyter notebooks, working with several interns on exploratory studies and publishing multiple papers on notebook usage and potential tooling. Two highlights were a large-scale study of publicly available open-source notebooks and an observational study of fine-grained notebook changes, for which we collected and analyzed a dataset at near-keystroke granularity.

Alongside this project, I became increasingly involved in machine learning for software engineering. In 2023, I led a Kotlin ML initiative that produced a suite of datasets, benchmarks, and fine-tuned models for Kotlin. We later explored using the Kotlin compiler in an RLVR setting to improve internal code completion models.
Drawing on my background in social sciences, I also briefly worked as a researcher in the Intelligent Collaboration Tools Lab and helped kick-start the Human–AI Experience Lab, contributing to several studies.
